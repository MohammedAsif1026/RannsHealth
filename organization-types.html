Physician Practices

Independent Practices · Single & Multi-Specialty Medical Groups · Ambulatory Clinics

Independent and group physician practices operate in high-pace clinical environments where rapid cash acceleration, low administrative overhead, and dependable billing turnaround are paramount. We support physician practices with agile front-end verification, charge integrity audits, and dedicated A/R resolution.

Rapid Pre-Service Eligibility
Real-time insurance coverage validation and co-pay calculation prior to patient arrival to minimize front-end denials.
Charge Capture Integrity
Systematic encounter reconciliation against provider schedules to identify missing charges and uncoded procedures.
Fast Clean Claim Filing
Automated claim scrubbing against national and local payer edits, accelerating remittance turnaround and reducing rework.
Patient Balance Engagement
Clear, transparent patient statements and structured payment workflows to improve self-pay collections.

Hospitals & Health Systems

Acute Care Facilities · Integrated Delivery Networks (IDNs) · Academic Medical Centers

Enterprise health systems manage complex multi-departmental revenue cycles characterized by inpatient DRG groupers, outpatient prospective payments (OPPS), charge master (CDM) governance, and cross-facility EHR integrations. RannsHealth delivers enterprise-grade scalability, denial recovery intelligence, and chart review automation.

Complex Inpatient & Outpatient Billing
Comprehensive support for UB-04 / CMS-1450 forms, revenue code mappings, and DRG validation.
Enterprise Denial Root-Cause Routing
Algorithmic categorization of payer denials across clinical necessity, timely filing, and medical record requests.
Cross-Platform EHR Harmonization
Seamless interface bridges between enterprise EHRs (Epic, Cerner, MEDITECH) and billing clearinghouses.
AI Chart Review & Extraction
Integration with rannsCCR and rannsCDE to rapidly parse high-volume operative notes, discharge summaries, and inpatient charts.

Community Health Centers

FQHCs (Federally Qualified Health Centers) · FQHC Look-Alikes · Rural Health Clinics (RHCs)

Community health centers and FQHCs fulfill an essential public mission requiring specialized billing rules, including Prospective Payment System (PPS) encounter rates, sliding fee discount schedules, Title X grant accounting, and annual Uniform Data System (UDS) reporting requirements.

PPS & APM Encounter Billing
Accurate capture of all-inclusive rate (AIR) codes, T-codes, and qualifying encounter criteria across Medicaid and Medicare.
Sliding-Fee Schedule Administration
Workflows aligned with federal poverty guideline (FPL) tiers, documentation verification, and nominal fee collection.
Medicaid Managed Care Wrap-Around
Tracking and reconciliation of state Medicaid wrap-around supplemental payments to prevent under-realization.
UDS & Grant Data Support
Reporting structures designed to facilitate annual Uniform Data System (UDS) Table 5 and Table 6 encounter submissions.

3
PPS Claim Submission
Filing of specific G-codes/T-codes to Medicaid and Medicare.
4
Wrap-Around Reconciliation
Auditing managed care payments and state supplemental claims.
