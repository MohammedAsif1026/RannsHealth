Behavioral Health Billing

Specialized claims generation and submission for behavioral health outpatient clinics, group practices, and residential centers across commercial payers, Medicare, and state Medicaid programs.

  • Electronic 837P and 837I transaction scrubbing
  • Authorized session units and date span management
  • Payer-specific billing guideline mapping

Mental Health Billing

Precise billing for individual, group, and family psychotherapy sessions, ensuring exact alignment between session duration documentation and time-based CPT code requirements.

  • Routine psychotherapy codes (90832, 90834, 90837)
  • Interactive complexity (+90785) add-on billing
  • Crisis psychotherapy session tracking (90839, +90840)

Psychiatry Billing

Tailored billing for psychiatric medical practitioners, combining medical Evaluation & Management (E/M) decision-making with psychiatric diagnostic evaluations and pharmacologic management.

  • E/M with psychotherapy add-ons (e.g. 99213 + 90833)
  • Psychiatric evaluations (90791, 90792 with medical)
  • Telepsychiatry POS 02 / 10 and modifier 95 tracking

Psychology Billing

Specialized coding for clinical psychologists conducting extensive psychological and neuropsychological assessment batteries, cognitive testing, and diagnostic reporting.

  • Psychological testing evaluation (96130, +96131)
  • Test administration by professional (96136, +96137)
  • Automated/technician testing code capture

Therapist Billing

Streamlined revenue cycle management for Licensed Clinical Social Workers (LCSW), Licensed Marriage and Family Therapists (LMFT), and Licensed Professional Counselors (LPC).

  • Couples and family therapy (90846, 90847)
  • Mid-level provider modifier assignment (AJ, HO)
  • Private practice & group clinic billing workflows

Substance Use Disorder (SUD) Billing

Navigation of American Society of Addiction Medicine (ASAM) criteria, Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) billing, ambulatory detox protocols, and residential addiction recovery claims.

  • MAT bundled weekly/monthly episode codes (G2086-G2088)
  • Presumptive & definitive drug screen billing compliance
  • Residential room and board revenue code coordination

IOP & PHP Billing

Billing management for Intensive Outpatient Programs (IOP) and Partial Hospitalization Programs (PHP), managing per-diem rates, minimum therapeutic hour thresholds, and UB-04 institutional claims.

  • Revenue codes 0905 (PHP) and 0906 (IOP) adjudication
  • HCPCS per-diem codes (H0015, H0035, S9480)
  • Concurrent authorization & length-of-stay tracking

CCBHC Billing

Expertise in Certified Community Behavioral Health Clinic (CCBHC) prospective payment systems (PPS), comprehensive community care coordination, and state Medicaid demonstration reimbursement models.

  • Daily/monthly PPS encounter rate reconciliation
  • Required 9 core services tracking & reporting
  • Quality bonus payment data integrity auditing

ABA Therapy Billing

Applied Behavior Analysis billing for autism spectrum disorder care, administering adaptive behavior assessments, direct 1-on-1 technician treatment, and Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) supervision.

  • Assessment & plan development codes (97151, 97152)
  • Adaptive treatment by technician (97153) in 15-min increments
  • BCBA protocol modification & supervision (97155)

Behavioral Health Credentialing

Provider enrollment, primary source verification, and panel credentialing for psychiatrists, psychologists, therapists, and behavioral health facilities across commercial and government health plans.

  • CAQH profile establishment and quarterly re-attestation
  • Commercial payer network enrollment & contract tracking
  • State Medicaid and Medicare PECOS enrollment
