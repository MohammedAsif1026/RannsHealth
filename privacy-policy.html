01. Purpose & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how [Company Legal Entity Name, e.g., RannsHealth / Infognana Solutions LLC] ("we," "our," or "the Company") handles information gathered through our public website, client inquiry portals, and introductory demonstrations of our healthcare Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) and AI software products (including rannsCDE, rannsCCR, rannsCPA, and the RCM Calculator).

This policy applies to website visitors, prospective healthcare clients, and authorized user representatives interacting with our web interfaces. It does not replace or modify binding Master Services Agreements (MSAs) or Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) executed with contracted healthcare provider clients.

02. Categories of Information We Process

We distinguish strictly between general website interactions and healthcare operations data:

  • Inquiry & Contact Information: Professional name, work email address, healthcare organization name, provider type, claims volume bracket, and stated evaluation objectives provided voluntarily via consultation booking forms.
  • Technical & Navigational Telemetry: Anonymized browser type, operating device characteristics, standard server access logs, and performance metrics utilized solely for web security and operational uptime monitoring.
  • Interactive Calculator Inputs: Aggregated monthly collection amounts and estimated denial percentages submitted through the RCM Calculator. These numeric inputs are processed in-session to generate mathematical ROI models and are not retained as identifiable patient records.

03. HIPAA Alignment & Business Associate Role

Where our operations involve the receipt, creation, maintenance, or transmission of Protected Health Information (PHI) on behalf of healthcare providers (Covered Entities), we act strictly in the capacity of a Business Associate governed by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health (HITECH) Act.

Website visitors are instructed never to submit actual patient names, social security numbers, or identifiable patient medical records via unauthenticated public web contact forms. Public web forms are intended exclusively for commercial business communications. Production PHI ingestion is restricted to authenticated, encrypted clearinghouse endpoints and dedicated integration gateways under executed Business Associate Agreements.

04. How We Use Information

Information collected through the website is used exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • Responding to demo requests, schedule consultations, and provide requested RCM performance evaluations.
  • Validating institutional credentials of prospective provider clients requesting access to technical architecture whitepapers.
  • Maintaining the integrity, availability, and cyber defense of our web infrastructure against automated abuse and unauthorized access.

05. AI Processing & Data Segregation Safeguards

Our AI Labs solutions—including rannsCDE (Document Extraction), rannsCCR (Cognitive Chart Review), and rannsCPA (Prior Authorization Engine)—operate under strict data containment protocols:

  • Zero Public Model Training: Client clinical documentation and operational claims data are never transferred to public third-party model developers or used to train foundation models outside the client's secure tenant boundary.
  • Automated De-Identification: Data extraction pipelines incorporate automated PHI redaction and tokenization filters designed to mask direct patient identifiers during analytical processing.
  • Ephemeral Processing: In-session document demonstrations utilize synthetic test fixtures or ephemeral memory caches purged upon task termination.

06. Information Disclosure & Authorized Subcontractors

We do not sell, rent, or trade business contact information or healthcare data to marketing third parties. Disclosures are strictly limited to:

  • Infrastructure Providers: Enterprise cloud hosting, VPC environments, and data center providers operating under formal confidentiality covenants and Business Associate Subcontractor agreements.
  • Legal & Compliance Compulsions: Where required by valid subpoenas, court orders, or applicable healthcare regulatory authorities in accordance with statutory procedures.

07. Safeguards, Data Retention & Review Placeholders

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal and business information. These include end-to-end transport layer encryption (TLS 1.3), encryption at rest (AES-256), and role-based access restrictions.

Website lead submissions are retained only for the duration necessary to satisfy the commercial inquiry or ongoing business relationship, subject to corporate record retention policies ([Record Retention Schedule Period, e.g., 3 Years]).
