Clinical Capabilities

Comprehensive Chart Intelligence Suite

AI-Powered Patient Chart Search
Natural language queries retrieve historical physician progress notes, lab trajectories, and pathology reports in under 1 second.
Clinical Chart Summarization
Synthesizes multi-specialty encounters into structured, chronological patient briefs with clickable evidence back-links.
HEDIS Measure Extraction
Automated audit of clinical encounters against NCQA HEDIS quality specifications, identifying compliance and documentation gaps.
Gap Finder (Care Gaps)
Surfaces overdue screenings, missing diabetic monitoring exams, missed vaccines, and non-adherent medication patterns.
ICD-10 & CPT Intelligence
Cross-references physician clinical documentation against reported codes to eliminate medical necessity denials and under-coding.
Risk Adjustment (HCC) Support
Maps documented chronic conditions to CMS-HCC V24 and V28 categories, ensuring complete Risk Adjustment Factor (RAF) capture.
Medication & Diagnosis Search
Tracks medication therapies, dosage changes, adverse reaction notes, and active comorbidities chronologically.
Clinical Narrative Understanding
Decodes specialized medical shorthand, complex physician prose, diagnostic radiology impressions, and surgical notes.
EHR Integration (FHIR Compatible)
Bi-directional SMART-on-FHIR and HL7 connectivity enables live integration into Epic, Cerner, MEDITECH, and AthenaClinicals.
