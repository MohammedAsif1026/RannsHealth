Enterprise Healthcare Capabilities

Document Classification
Multi-modal AI classifies intake faxes, clinical progress notes, CMS forms, and lab reports with context awareness.
OCR for Scanned & Handwritten Docs
Proprietary vision models trained on cursive physician handwriting, degraded faxes, and historical carbon copies.
Forms & Claims Processing
Precision coordinate extraction for CMS-1500, UB-04, superbills, and custom clinical assessment questionnaires.
PHI Detection & Redaction
Automated detection and redaction of 18 HIPAA Safe Harbor identifiers to safeguard patient privacy at ingestion.
Intelligent Data Extraction
Named-entity recognition converts clinical text into structured ICD-10, CPT, HCPCS, NPI, and NDC terminology.
Human-in-the-Loop Validation
Confidence-scored routing directs ambiguous or low-threshold extracts to specialized verification queues.
Validation & Quality Control
Multi-stage business logic checks cross-field consistency between demographic data, diagnosis codes, and policy rules.
EHR, RCM & Analytics Integration
Native FHIR, HL7, and API pipelines synchronize extracted data directly into Epic, Cerner, Athenahealth, and billing systems.
Enterprise Security & Compliance
SOC 2 Type II certified, HIPAA compliant, with AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
